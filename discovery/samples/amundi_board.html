A variety of job openings

From internships to positions at all levels of seniority

Log on to jobs.amundi.com to find we offer hundreds of job openings:

  • 6-month internships, mostly for gap years or final studies in all our countries
  • 12-to-36-month work-study programs and apprenticeships (mainly in France)
  • 12-to-24-month International Internship placements (VIE – Volontariat International en Entreprise: work-study programs out of France)
  • entry-level positions on fixed-term and permanent contracts
  • positions at all levels of seniority, on fixed-term and permanent contracts.
     

A search engine allows you to sort listings by keywords, job type, contract type, and geographic area.
Sign up and set up alerts to be immediately notified when new opportunities are posted!

The Application Process

You have applied on jobs.amundi.com, your application is reviewed by our Human Resources specialists.
They may invite you to take online tests (verbal, logical, and linguistic skills). No preparation is required, but you might want to ensure you will not be disturbed and can rely on a stable connection.
Successful completion of the tests grants you access to different types of interviews with

  • HR representatives,
  • Your potential manager
  • Potential team members

depending on the type of contract you are applying for.

Should your application be successful, you will become an Amundian and join our shared development project!
